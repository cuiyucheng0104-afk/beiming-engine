# FACTS — 片中每一句陈述的出处

| 片中文字 | 出处 | 原文（引用） |
|---|---|---|
| 开场钩子：鲍鱼壳 80% 带砸痕（第 4 帧黄块，0–4 s 左上数据条） | Riedman & Estes 1990, Biological Report 90(14), p.33 | 同下“八成”一行；8.5 s 在原文页上回读这个数 |
| 它会用工具（“石头 rock = anvil”：胸口的石头，含 2× 局部放大） | Kenyon 1969, North American Fauna 68, p.84, 图 45 图注 | “A captive adult male sea otter from Amchitka Island pounds a clam, held between his forepaws, against a rock resting on his chest.” |
| 它会用工具（补充） | Riedman & Estes 1990, Biological Report 90(14), p.33 | “The most common form of tool use occurs when an otter places a rock on its chest while floating on its back and pounds hard-shelled prey … against the rock as an anvil” |
| 鲍鱼壳 八成带砸痕 / ~80% of abalone shells were hit | 同上 p.33 | “About 80% of the abalone shells examined by Ebert (1968a) in California had breakage patterns suggesting that they had been struck by tool-using otters.” |
| 女儿砸法 随妈妈 / Daughters use mom's method | 同上 p.35 | “Observations of tagged weaned pups and juvenile offspring of tagged females indicate that juvenile females may in fact forage on the same types of prey, and employ the same tool use method and use similar foraging strategies as their mothers” |

措辞边界：
- “八成”是 Ebert (1968a) 在加州检查的鲍鱼壳样本，原文是“breakage patterns suggesting”（痕迹提示），字幕写“带砸痕”，英文写 were hit；不扩大为“所有海獭”。
- 答案只说“砸法和妈妈一样”（原文 employ the same … method … as their mothers，且主语是 juvenile females），不写“妈妈教的”。问句“它从哪学的？”是提问，答案停在文献能支持的程度。
- Kenyon 图 45 是 1969 年阿姆奇特卡岛来的圈养个体，片中署名条写“1969 · 圈养个体”。
- 钩子与“咬不动”两张照片是蛤蜊进食照（BLM），片中没有声称照片里有石头；钩子上的 80% 指鲍鱼壳样本，不是照片里这只。
- 2.5 s 与片尾的海獭群照片（Sea otter float，USFWS 2018）只表现“仰面漂浮、胸口朝天”，不声称它们在用工具。
- 定格画面（断奶期母子）与答案镜头（幼崽躺在妈妈胸口）是示意，不是文献中被跟踪的那几只。
